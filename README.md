# PowerpostFit — 7-Figure Business Plan Dashboard

An interactive, single-file dashboard for the **PowerpostFit** fitness & posture
dropship → private-label business plan (2025–2027).

## View it

Open `index.html` in any browser — no build step or server required.
Charts are rendered with [Chart.js](https://www.chartjs.org/) loaded from a CDN.

## What's inside

- **Headline KPIs** — Year 1 revenue target, total SKUs, blended gross margin, startup budget
- **Market opportunity** — posture correction & e-commerce fitness market sizing
- **36-month revenue & net profit projection** (line chart)
- **Product catalog** — 50 SKUs across 6 categories with margins, viral score & tier
- **3-year P&L** — COGS 32% → 28%, ending at 43.4% net margin
- **Marketing channel performance** — target ROAS vs budget % (bar chart)
- **Growth roadmap** — 3 phases from foundation to $1M+ run rate
- **Risk analysis & mitigation** matrix
